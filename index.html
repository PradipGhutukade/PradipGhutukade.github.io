My role. 3D Product Animator

Project description

Created a premium 3D product animation for an official ISRO Series Ball Pen using Blender.

The project focused on showcasing the pen's design, materials, branding, and fine details through realistic lighting, smooth camera movements, and high-quality rendering. Every shot was designed to present the product in a clean and visually engaging way for marketing and promotional use.

  • Product Visualization

  • Camera Animation
  • Rendering & Post-Processing
  • Marketing-Ready Product Video
